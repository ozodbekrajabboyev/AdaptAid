# Appendices

## A. Learning-preference questionnaire

Twenty quick either/or questions. There are no right answers; pick the option closer to you. AdaptAid uses your answers as a starting point and adjusts as it sees what actually works for you.

_Written for AdaptAid on the four Felder–Silverman dimensions. It is not the official Index of Learning Styles; students who have taken the ILS can enter their scores instead._

| # | Dimension | Prompt | Option a | Option b | a points to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ar | When I learn a new algorithm, I'd rather… | try coding it straight away | read it through and think about how it works first | active |
| 2 | ar | In a study group I usually… | listen and think before I say anything | jump in and try ideas out loud | reflective |
| 3 | ar | When I get stuck on a problem, I tend to… | try different things until something works | stop and think it through carefully | active |
| 4 | ar | I remember things better when I… | have thought them over on my own | have done something with them | reflective |

| 6 | si | I prefer explanations that… | start from a concrete example | start from the general principle | sensing |

Scoring: each answer is a vote of +1 or −1 for one pole; a dimension's score is the mean of its five votes (−1 to +1).

## B. Pilot consent text (default; edit in Admin → Pilot study)

AdaptAid is part of a student research project. If you take part, we record your quiz answers, which teaching aids you open and your survey answers. Results are reported only as group statistics with pseudonymous IDs. Taking part is voluntary, does not affect your grades, and you can stop at any time.

## C. System Usability Scale and satisfaction items

1. I think that I would like to use AdaptAid frequently.
2. I found AdaptAid unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought AdaptAid was easy to use.
4. I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use AdaptAid.
5. I found the various functions in AdaptAid were well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in AdaptAid.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use AdaptAid very quickly.
8. I found AdaptAid very cumbersome to use.
9. I felt very confident using AdaptAid.
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with AdaptAid.

Scale: Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree. Odd items: answer - 1. Even items: 5 - answer. Sum x 2.5 gives 0-100; 68 is the usual average.
